Refunds

Last updated: 1 Nov 2026

Pebblemorph can be tried free, with every feature, for 30 days: the best way to see whether it does what you need before paying.

Within 14 days, for any reason

If you change your mind within 14 days of a payment (a licence, a renewal or an extra computer), write to support@pebblemorph.com with the email address you bought with. The refund goes back to the same payment method through Lemon Squeezy, and the licence stops working on your computers.

Yearly licence

You can cancel the renewal at any time from the link in your receipt; the licence then works until the end of the year you paid for. A renewal can be refunded within 14 days like any other payment.

Right of withdrawal

Under EU law the right of withdrawal from digital content ends once its delivery starts with your consent, which you give at checkout. The 14 days above apply all the same.
