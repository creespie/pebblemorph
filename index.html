Safe with your files

A program that touches your files has to be careful. These are the rules Pebblemorph follows.

Your files never leave your computer

Every conversion happens on your Mac or PC. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

Originals are never lost

The original goes to the Trash, where you can put it back, or stays next to the new file if you prefer. Nothing is overwritten.

Sensitive documents are never rewritten

Signed, certified or password-protected PDFs and archival PDF/A are not compressed, and you see why. If you choose to turn PDFs into another format, the original of such a document stays next to the new file.

Nothing half-finished

A download is converted only once it has finished. A file that can't be converted cleanly stays as it was, and you see why.

No account, no tracking, no ads

Over the internet goes only the licence check, about once a week: the code and your computer's name. During the trial, nothing at all, unless you ask it to install LibreOffice.

Built on tools the world relies on

FFmpeg, LibreOffice, Pillow and other open-source projects do the converting. Their licences are inside the app.

Questions

What happens when the trial ends?

Pebblemorph stops converting and leaves your files as they are. Your settings stay. Enter a licence code and it carries on where it stopped.

Does it need the internet?

No, it converts offline. With a licence it checks in with the store about once a week; if it can't reach it, it keeps working.

Can I use it on both Mac and Windows?

Yes. One licence covers up to 3 computers in any mix. You can free a computer from the app and use the place on another.

It converted something I wanted to keep as it was.

Put the original back from the Trash. In the settings you can set any kind of file to “Keep as is”, or keep every original next to the new file.
