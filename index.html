Safe with your files

A program that touches your files has to be careful. These are the rules Pebblemorph follows.

Your files never leave your computer

Every conversion happens on your Mac or PC. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

Originals are never lost

The original goes to the Trash, where you can restore them, or stays next to the new file if you prefer. Nothing is overwritten.

Sensitive documents are never rewritten

Signed, certified or password-protected PDFs and archival PDF/A are not compressed. If you choose to turn PDFs into another format, the original remains untouched.

Nothing half-finished

In case of an unsuccessfull conversione, the original file remains untouched.

No account, no tracking, no ads

Only the licence check goes over the internet, about once a week: the code and your computer's name. During the trial, nothing at all, unless you ask it to install LibreOffice.

Built on tools the world relies on

FFmpeg, LibreOffice, Pillow and other open-source projects do the converting. Their licences are inside the app.

Magic folders

Any folder can work like your Downloads: whatever you put in it is converted on its own, as soon as it lands.

  1. Make a folder

    For example “To convert” on your desktop, or the folder your scanner saves into.

  2. Add it to Pebblemorph

    Open Watching, click Add a folder and pick it. Add as many as you like.

  3. Drop files into it

    Drag them in or save them there: each one is converted as soon as it lands, with your settings.

Every folder follows the same settings. Files already in a folder when you add it are left as they are: for those, there's Convert now. Only files placed straight in the folder are converted, not those in folders inside it. It works while Pebblemorph is running, even with its window closed.

Questions

What happens when the trial ends?

Pebblemorph stops converting and leaves your files as they are. Your settings are kept. Enter a licence code and it picks up where it left off.

Does it need the internet?

No, it converts offline. With a licence it checks in with the store about once a week; if it can't reach it, it keeps working.

Can I use it on both Mac and Windows?

Yes. One licence covers up to 3 computers in any mix. You can free a computer from the app and use that slot on another.

It converted something I wanted to keep as it was.

Restore the original from the Trash. In the settings you can set any kind of file to “Keep as is”, or keep every original next to the new file.
